THE ONE WHO KEEPS GOING

Raat gehri ho, screen roshan rahe,
Khwaab bade hon, par kadam bhi chalte rahe.
Kabhi code mein uljhan, kabhi raaste anjaan,
Phir bhi rukta nahi — yehi hai uski pehchaan.

Python se shuru kiya, AI tak safar bana,
Har naye concept mein ek naya asar mila.
Kabhi Java, kabhi DSA, kabhi LangChain ka jahaan,
Seekhne ki bhookh ne banaya usko alag insaan.

Log kehte hain, “Itna kyun seekhte ho?”
Woh kehta hai, “Abhi toh bas shuru kiya hai.”
Girkar bhi khud ko dobara sambhal leta hai,
Har mushkil ko ek lesson bana leta hai.

Uske sapne sirf sapne nahi,
Unke peeche mehnat ki kahani hai.
Aaj chahe duniya use na pehchaane,
Kal uski pehchaan uski apni nishani hai.

Na shortcut chahiye, na aasaan raasta,
Bas ek mauka aur ek khula aasman.
Jo kal se behtar aaj ko banata rahe,
Wahi ek din apna kal khud likhta rahe.

Aur agar kabhi thak jaaye ye safar,
Toh ek baat yaad rakhna —
Manzil unhi ko milti hai aksar,
Jo rukne ke baad bhi kehte hain:
“Chal, ek baar aur try karte hain.”

— For the one who keeps building himself
